## Figure, control, metric, and unit inventory

### Experiment figures

1. Deterministic baseline: body-component 3D vector and NED-component 3D vector.
2. Yaw sweep: horizontal N/E vectors and track/flight-path angles versus yaw.
3. Sideslip sweep: body lateral velocity and air-relative track versus sideslip.
4. Broken transform direction: correct/broken horizontal vectors and NED component bars.

All velocity axes use `m/s`; all angular axes and labels use `deg`; NED Down-positive axes are named and drawn with positive Down visually downward.

### Interactive views and controls

- Views: body vector, NED vector, grouped body/NED component bars, and a textual metric summary.
- Speed `V`: 20 to 200 m/s.
- Angle of attack `alpha`: -15 to 20 deg.
- Sideslip `beta`: -20 to 20 deg.
- Roll `phi`: -60 to 60 deg.
- Pitch `theta`: -45 to 45 deg.
- Yaw `psi`: -180 to 180 deg.

Every slider has both changing and changed callbacks. The UI ranges stay inside the model's principal air-data and 3-2-1 Euler charts.

### Model and experiment metrics

- Aircraft air-relative speed, wind/body/NED velocity components, and horizontal speed: m/s.
- Angle of attack, sideslip, roll, pitch, yaw, air-relative track, flight-path angle, and broken-case angular separation: deg.
- Norm and round-trip errors: m/s.
- DCM orthogonality and determinant errors: dimensionless.
- `trackDefined`: logical guard for a near-vertical vector.
