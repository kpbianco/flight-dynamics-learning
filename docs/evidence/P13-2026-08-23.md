## Implemented model contract

Public inputs:

- outer altitude-to-pitch gain in `[0,0.008] rad/m`, baseline `0.004 rad/m`;
- scheduled unsaturated closed-loop pitch natural frequency in `[1.2,3.6] rad/s`, baseline
  `2.4 rad/s`;
- altitude feedback sign `+1` for correct negative feedback or `-1` for the deliberately broken
  case.

Fixed declarations:

- geometric altitude begins at `1000 m`; command steps to `1030 m` at `1 s`;
- airspeed remains fixed at `60 m/s`, which assumes unmodeled propulsion/energy support;
- pitch and flight-path angles are perturbations about level trim; positive values are nose-up and
  climbing;
- `Down=-h`, `Down_command=-h_command`, `e_D=Down_command-Down`, and correct altitude error
  `e_h=-e_D=h_command-h`;
- outer command `theta_c=sat(s*K_h*e_h,+/-10 deg)`;
- inner command
  `u_theta=sat(K_p*(theta_c-theta)+K_ff*theta_c-K_q*q,+/-20 deg)`, with positive `u_theta`
  declared as a generalized nose-up pitch-control effect rather than a physical surface sign;
- pitch plant `theta_dot=q` and
  `q_dot=-a_theta*theta-a_q*q+b_u*u_theta`, where `a_theta=0.8 1/s^2`, `a_q=1.2 1/s`, and
  `b_u=12 1/s^2`;
- fixed damping ratio `zeta=0.8` and visible gain schedule
  `K_p=(omega_n^2-a_theta)/b_u`, `K_ff=a_theta/b_u`, and
  `K_q=(2*zeta*omega_n-a_q)/b_u`, yielding the declared unsaturated pitch response;
- path approximation `gamma_dot=(theta-gamma)/1.5 s` and kinematics
  `h_dot=60*sin(gamma) m/s`;
- explicit forward-Euler grid `0:0.02:30 s`, exactly 1501 samples and 1500 updates;
- no persistent/global state, dynamic allocation from inputs, asynchronous work, or external I/O.

The `20 deg` pitch-control guard remains inactive throughout the accepted public-input domain. The
learner-visible authority boundary is the active `10 deg` pitch-command envelope. The path lag is a
transparent surrogate, not identified lift, angle-of-attack, speed, propulsion, energy, actuator,
or aircraft dynamics.
