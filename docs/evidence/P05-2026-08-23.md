## Model boundary and equations

- Inputs are scalar CG position from `15` through `65% MAC`, horizontal-tail area ratio from `0` through `30%`, angle-of-attack perturbation from `-5` through `+5 deg`, and elevator perturbation from `-15` through `+15 deg`.
- Stations `h = x/c_bar` are measured aft from the MAC leading edge; `h_t = 3.5` is an `x/c_bar` station rather than a fraction constrained below one. Positive `C_m` and pitching moment are nose-up. Positive elevator is trailing-edge down.
- The deterministic baseline uses CG `30% MAC`, `S_t/S = 20%`, `delta alpha = +2 deg`, and zero elevator perturbation around P04's `q = 1325.00798531847 Pa` and reference alpha `3.41754527929804 deg`.
- Visible governing equations are `K_t = eta_t (S_t/S) a_t (1 - d epsilon/d alpha)`, `C_L_alpha = a_w + K_t`, `h_n = (a_w h_w + K_t h_t)/C_L_alpha`, `SM = h_n - h_cg`, `C_m_alpha = a_w(h_cg-h_w) + K_t(h_cg-h_t) = -C_L_alpha SM`, `delta C_m = C_m_alpha delta alpha + C_m_delta_e delta elevator`, `delta M = q S c_bar delta C_m`, and `alpha_abs [deg] = alpha_ref,P04 [deg] + delta alpha [deg]`.
- Visible constants are `h_w = 0.25`, `h_t = 3.5`, `a_w = 5.0 /rad`, `a_t = 4.0 /rad`, `eta_t = 0.9`, downwash gradient `0.4`, elevator effectiveness `0.6`, `S = 16.2 m^2`, and `c_bar = 1.5 m`.
- Recognizable baseline outputs are tail lift contribution `0.432 /rad`, total lift slope `5.432 /rad`, neutral point `50.8468336% MAC`, static margin `20.8468336% MAC`, `C_m_alpha = -1.1324 /rad`, `C_m_delta_e = -1.3824 /rad`, `delta C_m = -0.0395282169`, and `delta M = -1272.717434 N*m`.
- A `+2 deg` disturbance therefore creates a nose-down restoring moment in the baseline. CG at `50.8468336% MAC` is neutral; CG at `56% MAC` creates `+314.6053 N*m` reinforcing moment. With no tail and CG at `30% MAC`, the neutral point is the wing AC at `25% MAC`, `C_m_alpha = +0.25 /rad`, and elevator authority is zero.
- This is linear, stick-fixed, incremental static analysis. Every geometry has a separately retrimmed zero-perturbation reference. It does not compute absolute moment/elevator trim after changing geometry, stall/post-stall behavior, damping, dynamic stability, short-period/phugoid motion, structural/drag tradeoffs, or handling qualities.
