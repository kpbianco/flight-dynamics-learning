## Implemented model contract

The model owns one generic signed control-surface deflection state and exposes two public inputs:

- time constant `tau` in `[0.05,0.50] s`, baseline `0.18 s`;
- symmetric rate limit in `[20,120] deg/s`, baseline `45 deg/s`.

Fixed declarations:

- time grid `0:0.01:5 s`, 501 retained samples, 500 explicit updates per trajectory;
- symmetric position envelope `+/-15 deg`;
- commands `0 deg` until `0.5 s`, `+25 deg` until `2.0 s`, `-25 deg` until `3.5 s`, then `+5 deg`;
- visible teaching ledger `M_y=(80 N*m/deg) delta`, conceptually matching P09's internal body-y moment;
- complete recurrence:
  `delta_dot_raw=(delta_command_limited-delta)/tau`, rate clipping, explicit Euler update, then
  defensive state-envelope clipping. Because `dt/tau <= 0.2`, every accepted complete candidate is a
  partial move toward a limited target and the defensive post-update clip remains inactive;
- broken recurrence: identical command, grid, lag, rate limit, and moment gain, with target and state
  enforcement of the one position envelope omitted. The request-infeasibility diagnostic remains
  present in both complete and broken outputs.

The position ledger measures duration for which the *requested command* is infeasible, not physical
surface dwell, enforcement state, or contact load at a mechanical stop. Target saturation enforces
the declared envelope on this accepted domain; post-update clipping is retained as a defensive guard
for future state/grid/recurrence changes. Servo current, backlash, hinge moment, load-dependent
behavior, aerodynamics, sensors, controllers, and identified-aircraft fidelity are out of scope.
Direct P10-to-P09 composition is also out of scope because P09's public function accepts two scalar
pulse scales rather than an arbitrary moment history and uses a different time grid.
