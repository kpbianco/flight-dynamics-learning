## Implemented model contract

Public inputs:

- constant pitch-gyro bias in `[-0.5,0.5] deg/s`, baseline `0.20 deg/s`;
- deterministic accelerometer additive-error magnitude in `[0,0.5] m/s^2` three-axis vector RMS,
  baseline `0.15 m/s^2`.

Fixed declarations:

- grid `0:0.01:8 s`, 801 retained samples, and 800 explicit trapezoidal updates;
- body axes `x` forward, `y` right, `z` down; navigation frame North-East-Down; positive pitch
  nose-up; `g_n=[0;0;9.80665] m/s^2`;
- pitch-rate truth `q=10 sin(2 pi (t-1)/4) deg/s` inside `1<t<5 s`, zero otherwise;
- North coordinate acceleration `a_N=2 sin(pi (t-2)/2) m/s^2` inside `2<t<4 s`, with East and
  Down coordinate acceleration zero;
- gyro `q_measured=q_truth+bias`, with both truth and measured angle produced by the visible same-grid
  trapezoidal recurrence;
- complete ideal accelerometer `f_b=C_n_to_b*(a_n-g_n)` and measured output `f_b+eta_b`;
- fixed three-axis multitone error: row means removed, vector RMS normalized to one, then scaled by
  the selected lever. It is replayable teaching error, not a random draw, Gaussian process, PSD,
  standard-deviation measurement, or identified sensor data;
- broken accelerometer: identical grid, truth, attitude, gyro, and additive error, but gravity alone
  is omitted from specific force.

The model excludes quantization, saturation, scale factor, misalignment, bandwidth, vibration,
bias drift, calibration, latency, GPS, fusion, hardware identification, and aircraft/flight fidelity.

## Retained simulated reference outputs

The Python oracle in `tests/test_p11_module.py` does not execute, import, translate, or parse
`model.m`. It independently implements the declared schedules, integration, rotations, specific
force, and error normalization with Python standard-library arithmetic. Static artifact tests link
the MATLAB source to those declared equations and constants.

Baseline `model(0.20,0.15)` references:

- sample count/update count/horizon: `801` / `800` / `8 s`;
- peak true pitch rate: `10 deg/s`;
- peak true pitch angle: `12.732133646887217 deg`;
- final true pitch angle: approximately `9.65e-18 deg` in the independent oracle;
- peak North coordinate acceleration: `2 m/s^2`;
- final gyro-integrated angle error: `1.6 deg`, closing to `bias*time` at every sample;
- accelerometer additive-error vector RMS: `0.15 m/s^2`, with each body-axis mean near zero;
- supported-level initial ideal specific force: `[0;0;-9.80665] m/s^2`;
- ideal body-x specific-force range: `0` to approximately `4.112142008245035 m/s^2`;
- ideal body-z specific-force range: `-9.80665` to approximately `-9.124728616596029 m/s^2`;
- at `3 s`, positive `12.732133646887217 deg` pitch and `2 m/s^2` North acceleration produce ideal
  body specific force `[4.112142008245034;0;-9.124728616596029] m/s^2`, pinning the nonlevel DCM
  signs rather than relying only on orthonormality and self-consistent closure;
- every DCM is orthonormal with determinant one, and
  `C_b_to_n*f_b+g_n=a_n` closes within retained tolerance.

Sweep references:

- gyro bias `[-0.50,-0.25,0,0.25,0.50] deg/s` at fixed `0.15 m/s^2` error gives final angle errors
  `[-4,-2,0,2,4] deg`; all truth and complete/broken accelerometer histories remain fixed;
- accelerometer-error vector RMS `[0,0.05,0.15,0.30,0.50] m/s^2` at fixed `0.20 deg/s` bias gives
  the same observed vector RMS values; all truth, ideal specific-force, and gyro histories remain
  fixed;
- zero bias reproduces gyro-rate and integrated-angle truth exactly; zero accelerometer error
  reproduces ideal complete and broken specific force exactly; the joint zero corner is fully ideal.

Broken comparison:

- supported-level broken ideal output is `[0;0;0] m/s^2` rather than `[0;0;-9.80665] m/s^2`;
- complete-minus-broken output is the rotated negative gravity vector at every sample;
- the discrepancy magnitude is `9.80665 m/s^2` at every sample;
- additive error cancels from the discrepancy, proving that the broken comparison omits gravity and
  does not alter the error waveform;
- the smooth finite zero-at-rest symptom exposes confusion between coordinate acceleration and
  accelerometer specific force, not numerical instability.
