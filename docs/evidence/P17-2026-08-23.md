## Implemented model contract

Public inputs:

- residual INS North-acceleration bias in `[-0.08, 0.08] m/s^2`, baseline `0.04 m/s^2`;
- deterministic nominal GPS position-error RMS in `[0, 4] m`, baseline `1 m`;
- mode `+1` for gated fusion, `0` for INS-only, or `-1` for deliberately broken ungated fusion.

Fixed declarations:

- North is positive and the initial state is `0 m`, `20 m/s`;
- `dt=0.02 s`, horizon `60 s`, exactly 3001 samples and 3000 state transitions;
- truth acceleration is `+0.5 m/s^2` on `[5,15) s`, `-0.5 m/s^2` on `[25,35) s`, and zero otherwise;
- the INS acceleration is truth plus constant residual bias, with dead-reckoning limits
  `delta-v=b_a*t` and `delta-p=0.5*b_a*t^2`;
- GPS supplies exactly 60 fixes at `t=1:60 s`, never at `t=0`, using a fixed mean-removed,
  unit-RMS multitone error shape scaled by the selected RMS;
- the otherwise unchanged GPS stream adds a `+80 m` position outlier at `t=30 s`;
- prediction uses constant acceleration; an accepted residual `r=z_GPS-p_minus` applies
  `p_plus=p_minus+0.45*r` and `v_plus=v_minus+(0.12/1 s)*r`;
- correct mode accepts `abs(r)<=25 m`, including the exact boundary; broken mode accepts every fix;
- no random source, input-sized allocation, global/persistent state, file/network/device I/O,
  toolbox estimator, opaque solver, timer, worker, future, or background resource.

P11, P12, and P16 are conceptual prerequisites. `model.m` does not consume their runtime outputs or
claim a compatibility adapter.
