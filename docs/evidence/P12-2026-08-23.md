## Implemented model contract

Public inputs:

- constant body-x non-gravity specific force in `[0,3] m/s^2`, baseline `1.5 m/s^2`;
- heading in `[-180,180] deg`, baseline `30 deg`, positive clockwise from North toward East.

Fixed declarations:

- mass `1200 kg`, uniform gravity `9.80665 m/s^2`, initial altitude `1000 m`, and initial body speed
  `[60;0;0] m/s`;
- body axes `x` forward, `y` right, `z` down; navigation axes North-East-Down; fixed zero roll and
  `30 deg` nose-up pitch;
- analytic grid `0:0.02:6 s`, exactly 301 retained samples and 300 intervals;
- `C_body_to_ned` maps body components into NED and its transpose maps NED components back;
- `f_b=[f_x;0;0]`, `f_n=C_body_to_ned*f_b`, and
  `a_n=f_n+[0;0;g] m/s^2`;
- analytic constant-acceleration position/velocity histories and body velocity obtained through the
  declared inverse DCM;
- kinetic energy `T=0.5*m*(v dot v)`, altitude `h=-Down`, potential energy `U=m*g*h`, and
  mechanical energy `E=T+U`;
- frame-invariant non-gravity power `m*f_b dot v_b=m*f_n dot v_n`, closed-form work in the model,
  and an independently trapezoidal work reconstruction in `run_checks.m`;
- correct balance `E(t)-E(0)-work(t)` and deliberately broken `h=+Down` balance whose exact false
  residual is `2*m*g*(Down(t)-Down(0))`.

The model excludes aerodynamics, lift, drag, wind, propulsion-system and actuator dynamics,
attitude propagation, rotational energy, sensor error, estimation, control, identified vehicle
data, numerical integration fidelity, hardware timing, and flight fidelity.
