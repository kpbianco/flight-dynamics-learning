## Model boundary and equations

- Inputs are scalar positive-up geopotential pressure altitude from 0 through 20,000 m, local temperature offset from -100 through +100 K, and true airspeed from 0 through 1000 m/s.
- Pressure follows the dry standard atmosphere. The gradient troposphere uses `L = -0.0065 K/m` through 11,000 m; the lower stratosphere holds standard temperature at `216.65 K` through 20,000 m and uses exponential pressure decay.
- The local temperature offset is applied at a fixed pressure-altitude surface. It changes density and speed of sound but does not define a complete nonstandard weather column.
- Visible governing equations are `rho = p/(R T)`, `a = sqrt(gamma R T)`, `Mach = V/a`, `q = 0.5 rho V^2`, and `EAS = V sqrt(rho/rho0)`.
- Inputs outside the altitude, temperature-offset, or true-airspeed ranges raise named P03 errors. A defensive absolute-temperature guard remains after the bounded inputs. Arguments also require real, finite scalar doubles, and the accepted boundary combinations are checked for finite outputs.

## Figure, control, metric, and unit inventory

### Experiment figures

1. Deterministic baseline: normalized temperature/pressure/density ratios and dynamic pressure versus true airspeed at the selected air state.
2. Altitude sweep: temperature, logarithmic static pressure, density, dynamic pressure, and Mach from 0 to 20 km.
3. Temperature-offset sweep: density/dynamic pressure and speed of sound/Mach at fixed 5 km pressure altitude and 150 m/s true airspeed.
4. Broken constant-density assumption: correct local-density dynamic pressure versus the result from incorrectly freezing sea-level density at 11 km.

All altitude axes use `m` or `km`; temperature uses `K`; pressure and dynamic pressure use `Pa` or `kPa`; density uses `kg/m^3`; airspeed and speed of sound use `m/s`; Mach and normalized ratios are dimensionless.

### Interactive views and controls

- Views: standard-atmosphere density versus pressure altitude, standard temperature versus pressure altitude, dynamic pressure versus true airspeed, and a textual air-state/air-data summary. A separate selected-state marker shows the local temperature offset without presenting it as a column-wide weather perturbation.
- Pressure altitude: 0 to 20,000 m, positive up.
- Local temperature offset: -40 to +40 K at a fixed pressure-altitude surface.
- P02 true-airspeed magnitude: 0 to 350 m/s.
- UI profile grids are fixed at 21 altitude points and 15 speed points per update. Every slider has both changing and changed callbacks; changing callbacks use `event.Value`.

### Reported metrics
