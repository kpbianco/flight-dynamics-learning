## Model boundary, equations, and recognizable outputs

- Inputs are scalar aileron pulse `-5` through `+5 deg`, bank release `-10` through `+10 deg`, rudder pulse `-5` through `+5 deg`, roll decay rate `0.8` through `5 1/s`, stable/neutral spiral decay rate `0` through `0.05 1/s`, and Dutch-roll damping ratio `0` through `0.6`.
- Body axes are `x` forward, `y` right, and `z` down. Positive `p` and `phi` are right-wing-down; positive `r` and `psi` are nose-right; positive `beta = atan2(v,u)` is air-relative velocity toward body right. Positive aileron/rudder are operational commands that generate positive moments; no trailing-edge convention is implied.
- The declared teaching condition is `V0 = 60 m/s`, `qbar = 1325.00798531847 Pa`, `S = 16.2 m^2`, span `b = 10.9 m`, `I_x = 2500 kg*m^2`, `I_z = 4000 kg*m^2`, `C_l_delta_a = 0.08 /rad`, `C_n_delta_r = 0.10 /rad`, pulse duration `0.18 s`, and Dutch natural frequency `1.15 rad/s`.
- Roll impulse buildup is `L_delta_a = qbar S b C_l_delta_a`, `p(0+) = L_delta_a delta_a Delta t/I_x`, `p_dot = -lambda_R p`, and `phi_R_dot = p`. Therefore `p = p(0+) exp(-lambda_R t)` and the isolated bank increment approaches `p(0+)/lambda_R`; roll-rate damping alone does not command wings level.
- Spiral response is `phi_S = phi_S(0) exp(-lambda_S t)` with the P01 small-angle heading proxy `psi_dot ~= g phi_S/V0`. The implementation evaluates the integrated heading with `expm1` or a near-neutral series to prevent cancellation and underflow. Exact zero produces persistent bank and linear heading. A positive time scale beyond finite binary64 range is reported as `Inf` with an explicit representability flag while every history stays finite.
- Dutch roll uses a rudder angular impulse to establish `r(0+)`; the frozen-path teaching approximation is `beta_dot ~= -r`. The pair obeys `r_dot = omega_D^2 beta - 2 zeta_D omega_D r`. The diagnostic norm `E = 0.5(r^2 + omega_D^2 beta^2)` has `E_dot = -2 zeta_D omega_D r^2`; it has units `rad^2/s^2` and is not energy in joules.
- Fixed grids are `0:0.02:5 s` for roll (251 samples), `0:0.05:25 s` for Dutch roll (501 samples), and `0:0.25:120 s` for spiral (481 samples).
- Baseline recognizable outputs are `p(0+) = 2.69533336375 deg/s`, roll time constant `0.4 s`, 2% settling time `1.56480920217 s`, asymptotic roll-mode bank increment `1.07813334550 deg`, `r(0+) = 3.15859378564 deg/s`, Dutch damped period `5.55436093993 s`, amplitude retained per period `0.316715078722`, sampled peak `|beta| = 2.12919349399 deg`, spiral half-life `27.7258872224 s`, bank at 120 seconds `0.248935341839 deg`, and proxy heading change `31.0613521533 deg`.
- The time-scale ratios are `T_D/tau_R = 13.8859023498` and `tau_S/T_D = 7.20154855483`, making fast roll subsidence, oscillatory Dutch roll, and slow spiral motion visually separable.
- Every accepted ordinary response stays within the declared `15 deg` bank and `5 deg` sideslip learning bounds. The deliberately reversed spiral sign reaches `100.427684616 deg` at 120 seconds and first exceeds `15 deg` at the sampled time 44 seconds; values beyond the limit are labeled only as failure symptoms.
- This is a deterministic, decoupled, linear teaching model, not identified aircraft data, a complete lateral state-space/eigenvector model, handling-qualities evidence, a 6-DOF trajectory, or flight evidence.
