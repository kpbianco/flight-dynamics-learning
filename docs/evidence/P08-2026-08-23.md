## Model boundary, equations, and recognizable outputs

The deterministic teaching state is `x = [beta, p, r, phi]'`. Radians and radians per second are used internally. Positive `beta` is air-relative velocity toward body right; positive `p` and `phi` are right-wing-down; positive `r` is nose-right.

```text
p_hat = p b/(2 V0)                r_hat = r b/(2 V0)
CY = CY_beta beta + CY_p p_hat + CY_r r_hat
Cl = Cl_beta beta + Cl_p p_hat + Cl_r r_hat
Cn = Cn_beta beta + Cn_p p_hat + Cn_r r_hat

Y = qbar S CY                     L = qbar S b Cl
N = qbar S b Cn

beta_dot = Y/(m V0) - r + (g/V0) phi
p_dot = L/Ix                      r_dot = N/Iz
phi_dot = p
```

- Fixed condition: `V0 = 60 m/s`, `qbar = 1325.00798531847 Pa`, `S = 16.2 m^2`, `b = 10.9 m`, `m = 1200 kg`, `Ix = 2500 kg*m^2`, `Iz = 4000 kg*m^2`, and `g = 9.80665 m/s^2`.
- Fixed derivatives: `CY_beta=-0.65 /rad`, `CY_p=-0.03`, `CY_r=+0.25`, `Cl_beta=-0.12 /rad`, `Cl_r=+0.10`, `Cn_p=-0.06`, and `Cn_r=-0.25`. Learner controls are initial sideslip, `Cl_p`, and `Cn_beta`.
- Accepted controls: sideslip `-4` through `+4 deg`, `Cl_p=-0.8` through `-0.3`, and `Cn_beta=0` through `0.24 /rad`. Baseline values are `+3 deg`, `-0.50`, and `+0.18 /rad`.
- The explicit RK4 recurrence evaluates `x_dot=A*x` over `0:0.02:25 s`, exactly 1,251 samples. There is no state-space helper, eigensolver, matrix exponential, ODE solver, or adaptive work.
- At baseline, `b/(2V0)=0.0908333333333 s`; initial `Y=-730.5425 N`, `L=-1470.0763 N*m`, and `N=+2205.1145 N*m`; initial `beta_dot=-0.581347 deg/s`, `p_dot=-33.691667 deg/s^2`, and `r_dot=+31.585938 deg/s^2`.
- Recognizable sampled outputs are first sideslip zero crossing `0.54 s`, peak `|p|=3.901553 deg/s` at `0.26 s`, peak `|r|=7.230285 deg/s` at `0.42 s`, and peak `|phi|=1.643471 deg` at `0.66 s`.
- At every accepted `Cl_p`/`Cn_beta` endpoint combination, each sampled state envelope over `20-25 s` is less than `99%` of its envelope over `15-20 s`. This catches slow growth on the declared horizon without claiming an eigenvalue proof or stability outside that horizon.
- The `Cl_p` sweep `[-0.30,-0.40,-0.50,-0.65,-0.80]` changes only `A(2,2)` directly; peak roll rate decreases `4.927272` to `2.983204 deg/s`, while initial roll acceleration is unchanged because `p(0)=0`.
- The `Cn_beta` sweep `[0,0.06,0.12,0.18,0.24] /rad` changes only `A(3,1)` directly; initial yaw acceleration rises from `0` to `42.11458 deg/s^2` and first sideslip zero moves from `1.76` to `0.46 s`. At zero, later yaw remains through coupled `Cn_p p_hat`.
- The broken case treats `Cl_p` as if it multiplied dimensional `p`. The correct `A(2,2)` is `-4.25045 1/s`; the broken expression produces the SI numeric value `-46.79398` with incompatible `1/s^2` units. Their numeric-value quotient is `2V0/b=11.00917 1/s`, not a dimensionless multiplier. Peak `|p|` falls to `0.647583 deg/s` and peak `|phi|` to `0.184170 deg`; those are defect symptoms, not improved fidelity.
- The model is a constant-trim, linear lateral perturbation lesson with diagonal roll/yaw inertia and notional coefficients. It is not an identified aircraft, a handling-qualities assessment, a nonlinear 6-DOF trajectory, or physical evidence.
