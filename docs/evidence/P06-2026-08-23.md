## Model boundary, equations, and recognizable outputs

- Inputs are scalar elevator pulse amplitude from `-5` through `+5 deg`, airspeed perturbation from `-10` through `+10 m/s`, short-period damping ratio from `0` through `0.8`, and phugoid damping ratio from `0` through `0.3`.
- Positive elevator is trailing-edge down and positive pitch rate is nose-up. The baseline `-2 deg` trailing-edge-up elevator pulse lasts `0.18 s`; the pulse is collapsed to an angular impulse, so actuator travel during the pulse is not simulated.
- The P05 values retained visibly are `qbar = 1325.00798531847 Pa`, `S = 16.2 m^2`, `cbar = 1.5 m`, `C_m_alpha = -1.1324 /rad`, `C_m_delta_e = -1.3824 /rad`, and static margin `20.8468335788% MAC`. P06 declares the notional teaching value `I_y = 1800 kg*m^2`.
- Short-period buildup is `M_alpha = qbar S cbar C_m_alpha`, `omega_sp = sqrt(-M_alpha/I_y)`, `M_delta_e = qbar S cbar C_m_delta_e`, and `q(0+) = M_delta_e delta_e Delta_t/I_y`.
- Exact kinematics are `alpha_dot = q - gamma_dot`. The reduced fast view uses only the explicit frozen-flight-path approximation `gamma_dot ~= 0`, so `alpha_dot ~= q`; it does not present that approximation as an exact aircraft identity.
- With decay rate `lambda_sp = zeta_sp omega_sp` and `omega_d,sp = omega_sp sqrt(1-zeta_sp^2)`, the analytic response is `alpha = q(0+)/omega_d,sp exp(-lambda_sp t) sin(omega_d,sp t)` and pitch rate is its derivative under the reduced approximation.
- The phugoid is the standard transparent constant-`C_L` point-mass approximation `u_dot = -2 zeta_ph omega_ph u - g gamma`, `gamma_dot = (2g/V0^2)u`, `omega_ph = sqrt(2)g/V0`, and `h_dot = V0 gamma`. The factor two is retained explicitly as lift's `V^2` sensitivity.
- The phugoid speed, speed rate, flight-path angle, and altitude are closed-form vectors. The retained residual checks `u_dot + 2 zeta_ph omega_ph u + g gamma = 0`; a nonzero centered-difference behavior checks `h_dot = V0 gamma`; and the signed range metric is checked against both the altitude history and its `54.3569695780 m` baseline value. The independent oracle separately reconstructs every vector.
- Fixed grids are `0:0.02:8 s` for the fast response (401 samples) and `0:0.25:120 s` for the slow response (481 samples).
- Baseline recognizable outputs are `omega_sp = 4.50065851568 rad/s`, short-period damped period `1.49032244911 s`, initial pitch rate `8.90201844907 deg/s`, peak `|delta alpha| = 1.25625856215 deg`, `omega_ph = 0.231144957191 rad/s`, phugoid damped period `27.2702835663 s`, peak `|gamma| = 5.99082733388 deg`, altitude range `54.3569695780 m`, and period ratio `T_ph/T_sp = 18.2982438348`.
- The `0.18 s` pulse keeps all accepted ordinary elevator/damping combinations within P05's local `+/-5 deg` alpha domain; the worst accepted sampled peak is below `5 deg`. The broken positive-exponent trace reaches about `97.308 deg` in 2.5 s and is labeled only as an unmistakable invalid-model failure symptom, not a large-angle prediction.
- This is a deterministic, decoupled, linear, underdamped teaching model. It is not identified aircraft data, a full longitudinal state-space model, actuator dynamics, trim recomputation, handling-qualities analysis, or flight evidence.
