## Model boundary and equations

- Inputs are scalar air density from `0.04` through `2 kg/m^3`, true airspeed from `20` through `200 m/s`, mass from `400` through `3000 kg`, and flight-path angle from `-10` through `+10 deg`.
- The deterministic baseline uses P03's standard 5 km density `0.736115547399152 kg/m^3`, `60 m/s`, `1200 kg`, and `gamma = 0 deg`.
- Visible governing equations are `q = 0.5 rho V^2`, `W = m g`, `L = W cos(gamma)`, `T = D + W sin(gamma)`, `CL = L/(q S)`, `alpha = (CL - CL0)/CLalpha`, and `CD = CD0 + k CL^2`.
- The notional aircraft constants are `S = 16.2 m^2`, `CL0 = 0.25`, `CLalpha = 5.0 per rad`, `CD0 = 0.025`, `k = 0.045`, `CLmax = 1.4`, and an idealized constant thrust cap of `4000 N`.
- Recognizable baseline outputs are `q = 1325.0079853 Pa`, `W = 11767.98 N`, `CL = 0.5482371`, `alpha = 3.41755 deg`, parasite drag `536.628 N`, induced drag `290.324 N`, required thrust `826.952 N`, stall speed `37.5467 m/s`, and minimum-drag speed `51.4581 m/s`.
- The algebra reports finite requirements inside the learning domain without clamping. The lift-coefficient margin uses the equivalent form `CLmax [1 - (Vstall/V)^2]`, so the model's own analytic stall speed is exactly the inclusive zero-margin boundary while the adjacent lower representable speed remains infeasible. `trimFeasible` is true only when required `CL` and thrust also satisfy their declared limits.
- This is steady path-axis point-mass force trim. It does not solve pitching moment, elevator position, propulsion dynamics, full-aircraft trim, or static stability.
